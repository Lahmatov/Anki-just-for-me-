Recap turns what you watch and what you study in class into words that stick.

TV SHOWS AND MOVIES
• Ready word lists for the first seasons of 50 popular shows — built into the app, no connection needed.
• Mark the episodes you've watched: after each one, five words, three questions and a short retelling.
• Talk the episode over with Monchik, a friendly moose who corrects your mistakes kindly.

LESSONS
• Paste your lesson notes, take a photo of a textbook page or pick a PDF — Recap makes the cards: words, phrases and rules.
• Drills on the lesson's grammar and a lesson map that shows what you've already mastered.

REVIEW THAT WORKS
• FSRS spaced repetition: a card comes back just before you'd forget it.
• Recognition, typed translation, listening, spelling, fill-in-the-gap sentences and pronunciation.
• A daily goal in minutes, a streak and a Home Screen widget.

THREE LANGUAGES
English, European Portuguese and Italian — each with its own vocabulary and progress. The app speaks Russian, Portuguese and English.

NOTHING EXTRA
No ads, no tracking. Your cards and progress stay on your phone; a cloud backup is optional.

RECAP PLUS
The subscription unlocks word lists for any episode or movie, conversations with Monchik and cards from lessons without your own API key. Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the period in your Apple ID settings.
