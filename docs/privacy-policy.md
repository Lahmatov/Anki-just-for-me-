The policy now lives in [`legal/`](legal/) in three languages, together with the
terms of use and licenses. The same files are bundled into the app.

|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy Policy | [privacy.en.md](legal/privacy.en.md) | [privacy.ru.md](legal/privacy.ru.md) | [privacy.pt.md](legal/privacy.pt.md) |

Before publishing, replace `CONTROLLER_NAME`, `CONTROLLER_ADDRESS` and `CONTACT_EMAIL`
in the published copies. The app fills them from `RecapSellerName`,
`RecapSellerAddress` and `RecapContactEmail` in `project.yml`.
