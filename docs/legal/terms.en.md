## 1. What Recap is

Recap is an app for learning American English from TV shows: flashcards with spaced
repetition, pronunciation practice, retelling episodes and chatting about them.

Most of Recap is free and works offline. Some features use an AI model (Claude by
Anthropic). You can use them in two ways:

- with **your own Anthropic API key**. Requests then go from your phone straight to
  Anthropic under your own Anthropic account, and you pay Anthropic directly;
- with **Recap Plus** or a promo code. Requests then go through our server within a
  monthly allowance.
